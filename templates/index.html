Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions regarding admissions, documentation, or visas? Browse through our detailed answers to common inquiries.

When is the ideal time to initiate my study abroad application process?

We strongly recommend initiating your consultation and documentation process at least 6 to 8 months prior to your targeted intake semester (Fall or Spring) to secure admissions and limited scholarships effectively.

Are English proficiency tests like IELTS or TOEFL mandatory for all countries?

While major English-speaking destinations require standardized language proficiency tests, several European institutions offer alternative evaluation criteria or accept medium-of-instruction certificates under specific guidelines.

How can I monitor the live status of my university applications?

By logging into your exclusive personal account on our website's Student Portal, you can review real-time status updates, university offer letters, and immigration milestone progress 24/7.
